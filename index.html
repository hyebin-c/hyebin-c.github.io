About

I am a Ph.D. student in the MMLab at KAIST, advised by Professor Joon Son Chung, and an AI researcher at Boeing Korea. I received my M.S. from KAIST, where I was advised by Professor Munchul Kim in the VIC Lab. My research focuses on audio-centered AI, particularly multimodal language models and mechanistic interpretability.
